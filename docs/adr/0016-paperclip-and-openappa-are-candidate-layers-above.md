# 16. Paperclip and OpenAPPA are candidate layers above/inside zero, not replacements: Paperclip = orchestration (tasks/approvals/budget) driving an OpenShell sandbox via its SSH execution target

## Context and Problem Statement

ai-docs/tool-eval-paperclip-openappa.md.

## Decision Outcome

Paperclip and OpenAPPA are candidate layers above/inside zero, not replacements: Paperclip = orchestration (tasks/approvals/budget) driving an OpenShell sandbox via its SSH execution target; OpenAPPA = data-flow guardrail on tool calls inside the sandbox (opencode adapter). Neither is adopted now; evaluate after Phase 2.
